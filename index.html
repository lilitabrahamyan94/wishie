About us

Everything you want, in one place.

wishie is for people who love shopping online and shop in more than one store. Save a product from any shop with its link, and it joins a single list you can come back to.

For every saved piece we keep the photo, the price and whether it is in stock, so you can compare what you love side by side and buy when the moment is right, not when a sale banner tells you to.

We also gather promotions and coupon codes from the brands you follow, and we label advertising clearly. Your list is yours: it exists to help you choose well, not to push you to buy more.

FAQs

Answers to what people ask most.

Getting started

What is wishie?

A single place for products you want to buy from different online shops. You save a product with its link, and it joins one list with its photo, price and stock status.

How do I add a product?

Open the product’s page in the shop, copy the address from your browser, paste it into the “Paste a product link” field on the Wishlist page, and press the blue plus button.

Which shops can I save from?

You can paste a link from any online shop. How much detail we can show depends on the shop: some share photos, price and stock automatically, and some do not.

Is wishie free?

Yes. There is nothing to pay to save products or use coupons.

Your list

Why was my product added without a photo or price?

Some shops block automatic lookups of their pages. When that happens the link is still saved, and “Open at” the shop takes you to the product, but we cannot show its photo, price or stock.

Where is my list saved? Can I see it on my phone too?

Right now your list is saved in the browser you added it from, so it does not appear on your other devices and other people cannot see it. Signing in to keep one list everywhere is on the way.

I removed a product by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, if you are quick: an “Undo” link appears next to the link field right after you remove something. After that, add the product again with its link.

How do the filters work?

Each filter group lets you pick one option. Choosing a shop, a category, a price range and availability together narrows the list to products that match all of them. Pick the first option in a group to switch that filter off.

Prices and stock

How up to date are the price and stock?

Each product shows a “Checked” date. That is the last time we read the price and stock from the shop. Prices change often, so confirm the final price on the shop’s site before you buy.

What does “Was $99.00 (40% off)” mean?

It is the shop’s own earlier price and the discount the shop was advertising when we last checked. We show it as the shop states it and do not verify how long that earlier price was charged.

Will you tell me when a price drops or something is back in stock?

Not yet. For now, open your wishlist to see current details. Alerts are planned.

Can I buy products on wishie?

No. We do not sell anything. “Open at” takes you to the shop, and you buy there under the shop’s own terms, delivery and returns.

Coupons and ads

Are the coupons real?

Not yet. The coupons shown today are samples marked “Sample offer”, with codes that start with SAMPLE. They will not work at checkout. Real offers will replace them.

What do “Ad”, “Sponsored” and “Exclusive” mean?

“Ad” and “Sponsored” mark paid placements from a brand. “Exclusive” marks an offer that comes with a code for wishie users. Products you saved yourself are never ads.

Does wishie earn money when I buy something?

We may earn a commission from a shop when you follow a link and buy. It does not change the price you pay.
